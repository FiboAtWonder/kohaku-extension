## 1 · Asks — things their spec must guarantee

| ID | Ask | Why it blocks us |

Without these fields on the attempt event, D-13's countdown, the D2 owner banner, G-01's card, and any watcher alert have NO chain source at the hidden privacy levels — every timer surface we drew is unbuildable. Their own R-16 round defers exactly this question, so the window is open. Also covers naming who is recovering (D2-01/B-03 need the handover in the clear). |
| **REC-19** | **Define `slot`: within a clause, is it the clause index or the credential index?** (`design.md:172` — "which place in the rule this fills" — is ambiguous.) | The guardian payload spec and the D-07 checklist copy are held until answered. |
| **REC-24** | **A privacy level that publishes the shape but encrypts the values.** Their dial is three-way — `encrypt(password_key, config) | config | empty` — all-hidden, all-public, or nothing. Our middle level "Shape visible" (shape readable, values hidden) has no counterpart. Either they add the level or we ship without it. | Ruled 2026-08-25 (decision 97): **we assume all three of our levels will be available, carried on the contract’s event logs** (the setup event’s backup field — storage keeps only the commitment, per their I-16): for "Shape visible" the log carries the shape in the clear and the values encrypted. This ask confirms the kit builds that shape. **DEMOTED from load-bearing to opt-in (decision 99):** our default is now **"Private"**, which maps straight onto their existing encrypt-everything mode, so a negative answer no longer moves the default — it only drops "Shape visible", which ships **only if the kit builds the middle shape**. |

Their spec still scopes "the Ambire fork the demo integrates" as the one adapter owed, and their Q-19 still assumes an Ambire-fork account — that premise is stale: our A5 moved the demo substrate on 2026-08-20. Without the cause as data we must collapse four endings into one vague screen. |

A short window punishes exactly the slow, legitimate recoverer: the guardian who answers next weekend, the helper in another timezone, the user who needs two attempts at a proof. Every expiry we hand a real user is a re-gather round trip through people who already helped once. |

## 3 · Q-8 ownership answer — confirmed, ready to send (REC-18)

Rendering the disclosures is a **UX guarantee, not an SDK one** (Fibo, 2026-08-25). Trust-root declarations, tiers, and method metadata must arrive as SDK **data**; we reject "a conformance requirement the SDK checks" and any SDK verdict on the holder's rule quality (our A6). The line to hold, since the `sdk` is now a named module inside their invariant system (I-5, I-16): SDK checks on its own inputs are fine, SDK judgments about rule quality are not. Our proof of intent: the destination-key rendering and the adopted disclosure set live in our spec (decisions 96/98 — #4/#5/#6 MVP, #1/#2/#3/#7 V1).
